How it works

From spotted to safe, in minutes.

Anyone can report. Every report goes directly to verified rescuers nearby. No bureaucracy, no waiting.

Start a report →
01
Report the animal
Take a photo, add a short description, and submit. Your location is detected automatically — no address needed.
02
Get matched with nearby rescuers
ResQTrack finds verified NGOs, shelters, and vet clinics within range. The closest available responder gets notified instantly.
03
Track rescue status in real time
Follow the case from accepted → on the way → rescued. You'll see who's responding and where the animal is headed.
